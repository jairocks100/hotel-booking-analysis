Validation and export notes

Rows with missing/invalid required dates or room count, checkout on/before check-in, booking before signup, booking after check-in, or non-positive rooms are excluded. Price and stay-length mismatches are flagged but retained. Unknown booking statuses are excluded. Duplicate booking IDs are excluded after their first occurrence. Blank booking IDs are excluded.

CSV exports contain only booking ID, city, check-in date, booking status, weather values, and exposure flags. Names and contact details are not exported. IDs may still be sensitive; handle downloaded files accordingly. CSV formula prefixes are neutralized.
